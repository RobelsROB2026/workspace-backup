TYPE: Live hypnotherapy session — conversational pre-talk flowing into induction

THERAPIST: Before you experience hypnosis, I want you to experience what it feels like to be hypnotised before you actually go into trance. How is that?

CLIENT: Okay.

THERAPIST: That's good. I'm sure you've had that experience when you're driving a Car and it's a familiar route that you know well. And after a while, you can find your conscious mind drifting off...

[Extended driving metaphor — therapist describes the experience of highway hypnosis, the familiar route, the drift of conscious attention]

CLIENT: Yeah. It's like when…

[Client gives brief account of a similar personal experience]

THERAPIST: I'm going to make some suggestions to you as we go through this. The suggestions will be pleasant and peaceful...

CLIENT: Yep.

[Therapist tells anecdote about being at McDonald's and a waitress demonstrating hypnosis — uses the story as an embedded induction frame]

THERAPIST: Close your eyes. Close your eyes and keep them closed for 3 or 4 seconds. 1, 2, 3, 4. Open your eyes.

THERAPIST: That's exactly what hypnosis feels like. Did that feel strange to you?

CLIENT: No.

THERAPIST: Good. Now I want you to close your eyes again. And this time, I want you to take a deep breath in... and as you exhale, just let your body relax. That's right. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Open your eyes.

THERAPIST: Now, open your eyes. How was that?

CLIENT: It was good. Relaxing.

THERAPIST: It was, wasn't it? Now, this time when you close your eyes, I want you to imagine that you are in the most peaceful, calm place you've ever been. A place where you feel completely safe and completely relaxed. Close your eyes now.

[Extended deepening sequence — therapist guides client through visualization]

THERAPIST: Do you have a mental picture?

CLIENT: A Beach, where we've been on holiday.

THERAPIST: Good. Tell me about it. What can you see?

CLIENT: The sea. Sand. The sun is shining.

THERAPIST: What can you hear?

CLIENT: The waves.

THERAPIST: And what can you feel?

CLIENT: It's warm. The sun on my skin.

THERAPIST: That's wonderful. And what did you become aware of?

CLIENT: It's quiet. Warm. Just the sound of a few children playing. Apart from that, it's completely peaceful.

THERAPIST: That's right. And you can stay in that peaceful place. And every breath you take allows you to drift deeper and deeper into that feeling of relaxation. Each breath takes you further down. More and more relaxed. More and more comfortable. And with every word I say, you find yourself drifting deeper. And deeper. And deeper still.

[Therapist continues with progressive deepening — each cycle of eye-closing brings deeper relaxation, using fractionation technique of repeatedly opening and closing eyes with deeper states each time]

THERAPIST: And now, as you close your eyes for the final time, you go all the way down. Deep. Comfortable. Relaxed. And you know that you are safe, and you know that you are in control, and you can enjoy this feeling of total, complete relaxation.
