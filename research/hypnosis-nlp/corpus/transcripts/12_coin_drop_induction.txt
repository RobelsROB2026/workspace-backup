TRANSCRIPT: Coin Drop Induction
SOURCE: howtodoinductions.com
TYPE: Classical induction — fixation object with ideomotor signal
PARTICIPANTS: Hypnotist, Subject
FORMAT: Directive script with physical feedback (coin dropping as trance signal)
URL: https://howtodoinductions.com/inductions/coin/

---

[Subject holds a coin between thumb and index finger of right hand, arm resting on armrest or thigh]

HYPNOTIST: Now close your eyes and think of that coin between the thumb and index finger of your right hand… Now breathe in deeply and exhale slowly five times…

[Subject breathes — five slow breath cycles]

HYPNOTIST: Good. And as you continue to breathe, slowly and deeply, you can feel the oxygen circulating through your body. With each breath you take, you feel a wave of relaxation spreading through your body. Starting at the top of your head… flowing down through your face… your neck… your shoulders… down through your arms… all the way to your fingertips.

And as that relaxation spreads, you can notice something interesting about that coin. As your fingers relax… as your thumb and index finger become more and more relaxed… you may notice that the coin is beginning to feel different. It might feel heavier. It might feel like it's beginning to slip.

And when that coin drops — and it will drop — you'll find yourself going even deeper. Twice as deep. Three times as deep. The sound of that coin dropping is a signal to your unconscious mind to let go completely. To relax even further. To drift even deeper.

So just continue to breathe. Continue to relax. And let that relaxation flow into your fingers. Knowing that whenever that coin drops, you will go deeper into this comfortable, peaceful state of relaxation.

[Coin drops — subject's hand opens fully]

HYPNOTIST: That's right. All the way down. Deeper and deeper now. So relaxed. So comfortable. Just drifting. And floating. Every muscle in your body completely relaxed.
