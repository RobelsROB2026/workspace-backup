TRANSCRIPT: Dave Elman Two-Finger Eye Closure Technique

TYPE: Clinical induction variant — rapid induction with arm drop deepener
PARTICIPANTS: Hypnotist, Subject
FORMAT: Directive script with compliance testing

[Subject seated with arms resting on thighs, feet flat on floor]

HYPNOTIST: I want you to look up here at my fingers.

[Hypnotist holds index and middle fingers in a V-shape, approximately 12 inches above subject's eyebrows]

HYPNOTIST: Keep your eyes fixed on my fingers. As I bring my hand down, your eyelids will close.

[Hypnotist slowly passes hand down in front of subject's face]

HYPNOTIST: That's right, let those eyelids close down now. Good.

Now, I'd like you to relax the muscles around your eyes. Relax them so deeply, so completely, so profoundly, that they just won't work. And when you're sure they're so relaxed that as long as you hold on to that relaxation, they just won't work, test them to make sure they won't work.

[Subject tests eyelids]

HYPNOTIST: Good. That's fine. Now stop testing and just relax. Now, take that same quality of relaxation that you have in your eyelids and let it flow all through your body. From the top of your head to the tips of your toes.

Now I'm going to pick up your right hand by the thumb. When I let go, let it drop like a wet, limp rag. And as it drops, send a wave of relaxation from the top of your head to the tips of your toes.

[Hypnotist grasps subject's right thumb, lifts arm, lets it drop]

HYPNOTIST: Good. That's right. Now, I'm going to pick up your left arm. Same thing. When I let it go, send another wave of relaxation from the top of your head to the tips of your toes.

[Hypnotist lifts and drops left arm]

HYPNOTIST: Good. That's fine. And now I want you to begin a mental relaxation. In a moment, I'm going to place my finger on your forehead. When I do, I want you to begin counting backwards from one hundred. After each number, say the words "deeper relaxed." After every number, double your mental relaxation. Let the numbers begin to fade away. And by the time you've counted just a few numbers, they will simply disappear.

[Hypnotist touches subject's forehead]

HYPNOTIST: Begin.

SUBJECT: One hundred… deeper relaxed…

HYPNOTIST: Deeper.

SUBJECT: Ninety-nine… deeper relaxed…

HYPNOTIST: Let those numbers start to fade away now.

SUBJECT: Ninety-eight… deeper…

HYPNOTIST: Are the numbers gone?

SUBJECT: [nods or murmurs assent]

HYPNOTIST: Good. You're doing wonderfully. Deep, comfortable relaxation.
