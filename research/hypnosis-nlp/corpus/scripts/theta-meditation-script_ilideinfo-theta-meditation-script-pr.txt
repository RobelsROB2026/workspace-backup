THETA MEDITATION 


Creating a theta meditation is a 3 part process. You begin by bringing your client into a trance 
like state by lowering the brain wave activity to a theta state (Induction). You will then take 
them on a visualization journey you have created for them (Intention), and finish with arousal 
to bring them back out of the theta brain state. 


During Induction: 


Focusing on the breath. This brings awareness to the body, calms the nervous systems and 
begins to signal the brain its time to relax. 


Deepening Technique: You will cue your client to intentionally relax and soften the muscles of 
the body from one end to the other. We often don't notice the tension the hold with body parts 
until we focus in on it. 


** The purpose of this part is to bring your client into lower states of consciousness so the 
conscious mind softens and gives access to the subconscious mind where the beliefs, values, 
conditioning, old stories and disempowering limits are living. 


This will take roughly 3-4 minutes, sometimes longer. You can start with some breathwork or 3 
minutes of a kriya to drop your clients INTO their body and out of their mind before this part to 
really make this effective. 


During Visualization: 


Visualization: Once your client is fully relaxed, you can begin your suggestions or journeying if 
doing timeline work. Remember, the subconscious is highly metaphorical and symbolic, so the 
use of storytelling is very helpful. 


This is where it is important for you to know what your intention is for the session. You want to 
ensure you are gently taking your client's mind to memories, symbols, insight, guidance, or 
root stories can come to the surface. You may also use mantras, affirmations or visuals to 
evoke those positive thoughts. 


**NOTE: not all people are "visual" - the subconscious can speak many languages such as 


signs, symbols, sensations and more...so remind your client that however it shows up for 
them is perfect. 


During Arousal: 

When you are ready to complete your session: 

Gather up final bits of information, insight, guidance and surroundings. 

Slowly suggest the visuals fade into the background, bringing the client slowly out of the 


depths of the subconscious mind and back into their body. | also like to count backwards from 
10 to gently bring them into heightened awareness. 


Bring awareness to their physical body as it’s waking them back up through their fingers and 
toes. Let them take their time. 


Once they open their eyes, | offer them to share their insights with me or grab their journal 
while the thoughts are fresh.

PAST LIFE REGRESSION PROTOCOL 


Suggestion, Regression + Progression Protocol 


What is the difference between Suggestion, Regression and Progression Hypnosis? 


Suggestion Theta Meditation is when we are implementing the use of embedded commands or 
suggestions to our client’s unconscious mind to help them create or move towards a particular goal or 
outcome. Your client will most likely be in Light Theta state of consciousness during this type of 
session. 


Regression Meditation/Hypnosis is when we are providing limited imagery or suggestions and 
allowing the client to tap into a Past Life experience or a long forgotten memory from this lifetime in 
order to seek out a potential root cause for something challenging them in this lifetime or to create 
healing and wholeness within themselves by uncovering more of their innate wisdom and strength. 
Your client will most likely be in Theta state of consciousness during this type of session. 


Progression Meditation/Hypnosis is when we are providing limited imagery or suggestions and 
allowing the client to tap into a future time in this lifetime OR a future lifetime incarnation to allow them 
to witness what might take place. This type of theta meditation can be similar to placing a goal in the 


the timeline like you learned with Timeline Therapy with the exception that we are not painting the full 
picture but rather allowing our client to trust the vision that arises for them. Your client will most likely 
be in Deep Alpha state of consciousness during this type of session. 


You might be thinking that Regression + Progression Hypnosis sounds similar to guided meditation, 
and you would be correct! They are similar in that we are providing some guided imagery or 
suggestion, the difference is that we will be asking questions throughout the session. We might be 
asking therapeutic questions for healing purposes during a Regression session or we might be asking 
for descriptors in a Progression session for specificity purposes. 


A DISCLAIMER : Not everyone will be willing to move through a Regression session. They may 
consciously desire to do this but it may not be congruent with the subconscious, so just know that this 
is a possibility (as with all other hypnosis sessions) and that you can work towards congruence, if that 
is their choice, in order for them to be able to experience a Regression session.
