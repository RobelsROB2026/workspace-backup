Before you begin, loosen tight clothing and get comfortable. Close your eyes and start breathing deeply and slowly.

Continue this deep breathing about five or six times. With each exhale, your body relaxes further, becoming more calm, peaceful, and at ease.

Using your imagination, picture yourself lying on soft grass in a green meadow. The sun shines gently, and a light breeze flows across your body. Beautiful flowers bloom around you, swaying in the breeze. Notice their wonderful fragrance.

Now stand and look toward the beautiful mountain at the meadow's end. You notice a flowing stream to your right. Walk over, bend down, and place your hand in the pure, cool, refreshing water. Listen to the gentle rapids.

Follow the stream upward into the mountains. You reach a pond with warm water. Since you're an excellent swimmer, you enter the water briefly, feeling its warmth surrounding you refreshingly.

Climb higher. Hear birds chirping and smell pine trees. Halfway up, rest on a fallen tree, viewing the meadow below. Continue climbing as the breeze carries the scent of cedar trees.

You reach the summit, seeing a deep canyon on one side and the meadow far below on the other.

Begin descending. Notice deer in the forest and pause again at the fallen tree, watching the sunset form. Follow the stream back down through the beautiful landscape.

Return to the meadow and lie in the comfortable grass again. Smell the flowers' fragrance. You're now ready to receive additional suggestions before awakening.
