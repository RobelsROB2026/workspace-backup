**Hypothesis 1: Ericksonian hypnosis modulates affective/autonomic pathways but not nociceptive signaling.**
Across both the liver biopsy RCT and the bronchoscopy trial, hypnosis consistently reduced anxiety, cough, and dyspnoea but had a weaker or paradoxical effect on pain specifically. The sports injury study showed pain converging near zero, but only when combined with physical therapy. This suggests Ericksonian hypnosis acts primarily on top-down autonomic/affective regulation (vagal tone, HPA axis) rather than on peripheral nociceptive transmission — and that its apparent analgesic effects may be mediated entirely through anxiolysis rather than direct pain suppression.

**Hypothesis 2: The hypnotic context itself — independent of specific suggestions — triggers an endocrinological shift (cortisol down, oxytocin up) that accounts for the cross-domain efficacy.**
The Calm Contact technique paper found that hypnosis as a *setting* (not specific content) reduced cortisol and raised oxytocin. This would parsimoniously explain why the same Ericksonian approach works across radically different conditions (hypertension, eco-anxiety, bronchoscopy tolerance, MS hand function, sports injuries, psychosomatic disorders). The active ingredient may not be the indirect suggestions themselves, but the relational/attentional context that recruits oxytocinergic social-bonding circuitry — essentially a pharmacologically measurable "therapeutic alliance" effect.

**Hypothesis 3: Self-administered Ericksonian hypnosis via standardized recordings is non-inferior to practitioner-guided sessions for psychological (but not physiological) outcomes.**
The sports injury study achieved large effect sizes on stress, anxiety, and depression using pre-recorded audio without a live therapist, while studies targeting physiological endpoints (bronchoscopy tolerance, liver biopsy pain) all used live practitioner delivery. This suggests the indirect, metaphor-based style of Ericksonian hypnosis may be uniquely suited to scalable self-administration for psychological variables, but that real-time procedural contexts requiring acute physiological modulation may still need live delivery — possibly because the therapist's presence itself is part of the oxytocin-mediated mechanism identified in Hypothesis 2.
