## The Big Picture

Hypnosis is a **coordinated reconfiguration of three brain networks** (DMN suppression + ECN amplification + Salience Network coupling to ECN). It operates through the predictive coding framework — suggestions function as artificially precise Bayesian priors that override sensory evidence. This is now well-established across multiple imaging modalities.

But the field has several major blind spots.

## The 3 Most Important Discoveries

### 1. There Are Two Types of Hypnotizable People (and the field treats them as one)

Equally hypnotizable individuals with high vs. low dissociative tendencies show **opposite neural signatures** — literally opposite alpha power trajectories. The field's entire psychometric infrastructure (suggestibility scales) collapses these into a single score. This is like measuring "athletic ability" without distinguishing sprinters from swimmers.

### 2. The Label "Hypnosis" Does Most of the Work

White noise labeled "hypnosis" achieved 77% of real hypnosis's clinical effect on hot flashes. The word itself activates the network reconfiguration. This has profound implications for how we think about hypnotic language — the **frame is the mechanism**, not the content. No one has measured when the frame activates the networks relative to the content.

### 3. Nobody Has Computationally Analyzed Hypnotic Language

50 papers. Zero computational linguistic analyses of what makes hypnotic language hypnotic. The field studies what hypnotic language does to brains but not what it IS as a formal linguistic object. This is our biggest opportunity — it sits squarely at the hypnosis-NLP intersection.

## The Anomalies Worth Tracking

- **Sleep ≠ Anaesthesia**: Destroying 80% of sleep-promoting neurons produced anaesthetic resistance but no insomnia. Sleep and anaesthesia use different circuits despite looking identical on EEG. (PMC12106870)
- **The Reflex Paradox**: Hypnosis reduces pain perception while INCREASING spinal reflexes to the same stimulus. Consciousness and spinal cord are doing opposite things. (PMC12599604)
- **FND patients over-respond despite normal suggestibility**: 87% improvement rates, but they're not more suggestible. Their baseline network architecture (aberrant DMN-SaN coupling) may be pre-configured for hypnotic reorganization. (PMC12652343)
- **Gamma goes both ways**: Some studies show decreased gamma during hypnosis, others increased. Directional inversion in a supposed core neural marker. (PMC12595402 vs. Cardena et al.)
