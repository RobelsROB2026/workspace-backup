## I. THE NEURAL ARCHITECTURE OF HYPNOSIS

### 1. The Triple Network Reconfiguration

Hypnosis is a coordinated reconfiguration of three large-scale brain networks (Source: PMC13024316):

| Network | Direction During Hypnosis | Functional Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Default Mode Network (DMN)** | Suppressed (PCC, mPFC) | Silenced inner narrator, reduced self-referential processing |
| **Executive Control Network (ECN)** | Enhanced (DLPFC, posterior parietal) | Amplified top-down attentional control |
| **Salience Network (SaN)** | Coupled to ECN (ACC, anterior insula) | Redirected relevance-detection toward suggestions |

The magnitude of DMN deactivation correlates with behavioral measures of hypnotic depth (Source: PMC13024316, citing McGeown et al., 2009). The SaN acts as the switching mechanism between DMN and ECN — during hypnosis, it locks onto the ECN rather than oscillating freely (Source: PMC13024316). High absorbers show greater baseline DMN-ECN connectivity, facilitating rapid network reconfiguration (Source: PMC13024316). The ECN engages centrally in responding to suggestion, the SaN in altering attention, and the DMN in disengaging from self-referential thinking (Source: PMC12775527).

**Convergent evidence:** fNIRS data confirms increased bilateral prefrontal connectivity (especially BA46-BA46 interhemispheric DLPFC integration) during hypnosis (Source: PMC12178158). Heightened connectivity in the dorsal attention network, salience network, and somatomotor network during hypnosis further supports the three-network model (Source: PMC12178158, citing Vazquez et al., 2024).

**EEG microstate evidence:** Hypnosis increases EEG microstate A parameters (affiliated with DMN regions: frontal, left insular, occipital cortices) while decreasing microstate B (visual processing circuits). Post-hypnosis theta-band signals show decreased sample entropy (t = -3.35, P = 0.002), indicating a shift toward more ordered oscillatory patterns (Source: PMC12011983).

#### What This Looks Like in a Script

The **seven_plus_or_minus_two** script (Source: scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt) is the clearest practical implementation of DMN suppression. It deliberately stacks 9 concurrent attentional channels — voice, breathing, head weight, self-image, music, feet, arms, temperature, eyes — systematically filling working memory to capacity. Miller's 7±2 limit means the conscious mind cannot manage all channels simultaneously, forcing a collapse of the default mode's self-referential chatter:

> *"no need to think anything at all, really... no need to do anything... nobody wanting anything and nobody expecting anything"* (Source: scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt)

This triple negation cascade ("no need... no need... nobody wanting... nobody expecting") directly targets DMN activity by removing every self-referential goal structure. When the DMN has nothing to simulate about, it quiets. The script explicitly names the psychological mechanism ("in psychology, there's a rule called seven plus or minus two"), which itself adds another cognitive task — processing the concept — further saturating working memory and accelerating ECN-SaN coupling as the attentional system reorganizes around the hypnotist's voice as the sole salient input.

The **awareness induction** (Source: scripts/awareness_induction.txt) achieves DMN suppression through a different route — not overload, but systematic redirection. By guiding attention to hands, elbows, head weight, ankles, pulse, and residual tension, it replaces the DMN's default self-narrative with a stream of present-moment body awareness:

> *"Each beat of your heart sends a warm wave of relaxation"* (Source: scripts/awareness_induction.txt)

This pacing of attention to heartbeat is a direct anchoring of the SaN to interoceptive rather than self-referential signals — pulling the salience network into coupling with body awareness (ECN-mediated attention) rather than the DMN's narrative processing.

### 2. Predictive Coding Override

Hypnotic suggestions function as artificially precise prior expectations within the Bayesian brain framework (Oakley & Halligan, 2013; Source: PMC13024316). When a suggestion is delivered with sufficient contextual precision, it overrides bottom-up sensory evidence through top-down predictive signals. This explains:

- **Hypnotic analgesia**: Pain priors are reweighted to expect no pain → sensory pain signals are suppressed at the prediction-error level (Source: PMC13024316, PMC12250368)
- **Hypnotic hallucinations**: Visual/auditory priors are amplified → perception is generated without external stimulus (Source: PMC13024316)
- **Involuntariness**: Action priors bypass metacognitive monitoring → movements feel externally caused (Source: PMC13024316, PMC12358409)

Enhanced ECN-SaN coupling reflects strengthened top-down predictive signals, while DMN reduction reflects decreased interference from self-referential predictions (Source: PMC13024316).

**Critical nuance — imagery makes it real:** Mental imagery activates the same pain processing regions as actual pain experience — bilateral anterior insula, ACC, thalamus, S1. Scene-based mental imagery makes mentally represented events feel "real"; without imagery, verbal descriptions produce substantially reduced emotional responses (Source: PMC12994519). First-person imagery evokes greater pain modulation and right anterior insula activation than third-person imagery (Source: PMC12994519). This is the neurobiological basis for why vivid hypnotic suggestions work: they generate prediction signals strong enough to override sensory input.

**Predictive coding in FND and chronic pain:** Both functional neurological disorder (FND) and chronic pain involve aberrant predictions shaping bodily experience — expectations generating motor/sensory output independent of external stimuli (Source: PMC12258340, PMC12652343). FND symptoms and placebo effects both arise from abnormally focused attention modulating predictive models (Source: PMC12258340). The "predictive coding override" in hypnosis is not exotic — it is the same mechanism that produces FND symptoms, running in reverse under therapeutic control.

#### What This Looks Like in a Script

The **arm levitation method** (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt) is a textbook demonstration of predictive coding override. The script builds a sensory prediction through a 20-count numbered sequence, each number adding increasingly specific sensory detail:

> *"Number 1 — a tingling sensation in your right hand. Number 2 — the tingling spreads... Number 5 — your hand feels lighter... Number 10 — your hand begins to lift..."* (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt)

The numbered structure is not arbitrary — each number acts as a temporal anchor that creates an expectation of progressive change. By number 10, the brain's predictive model has been trained to expect upward movement. The culminating metaphor *"just as a gas-filled balloon will rise and float"* (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt) provides a physical-world analogy that makes the prediction concrete. The subject experiences genuinely involuntary arm levitation — not because the arm "wants" to move, but because the prediction of movement has become so precise that it overrides the prediction of stasis.

The **bilateral contrast** technique in the same script (left hand = "heavy as marble," right hand = "light as a balloon") creates opposing predictions simultaneously, which amplifies the salience of both. This is a direct exploitation of the SaN's role in detecting contrast — the prediction-error between the two hands makes both predictions more salient.

The **stiff arm induction** (Source: scripts/stiff_arm_induction.txt) uses the reverse-effort principle: *"The harder you try to lower it, the stiffer it becomes"* — a paradoxical suggestion that transforms the subject's own effort into confirmatory evidence for the suggestion's prediction. Every attempt to lower the arm generates proprioceptive feedback that the predictive model interprets as "stiffness increasing," creating a self-reinforcing prediction loop.

### 3. Frontal Decoupling (The Dissociation Engine)

Imaging consistently shows reduced functional connectivity between frontal executive regions and other brain areas during hypnosis (Source: PMC12129887, PMC13024316). This decoupling:

- Reduces agency monitoring (things "happen to you" rather than you doing them) (Source: PMC11852439)
- Diminishes critical evaluation (suggestions bypass rational filtering) (Source: PMC12767259)
- Creates controllable dissociation between executive systems and lower-level cognitive processes (Source: PMC12129887)

**fNIRS evidence:** BA45 (Broca's area homolog) consistently shows decreased connectivity with other PFC regions during hypnosis, suggesting reduced linguistic/analytical processing (Source: PMC12178158). Simultaneously, BA46 (DLPFC) shows increased interhemispheric connectivity — the executive control system integrates bilaterally even as it decouples from language-processing areas (Source: PMC12178158).

**The dissociation is measurable:** Frontal decoupling is not metaphorical. It corresponds to disruptions in ECN functional integration that can be quantified as altered agency experience (Source: PMC11852439). The think/no-think paradigm demonstrates that DLPFC-mediated inhibition of hippocampal retrieval is the mechanism underlying memory suppression — the same circuit that hypnosis modulates for post-hypnotic amnesia (Source: PMC12129887).

**Key nuance — two distinct neural subtypes:** Among equally hypnotizable individuals, there are at least two distinct EEG profiles. High-dissociation (HD) subjects show decreased alpha power and increased right-hemisphere gamma (imagery-like processing) during hypnosis. Low-dissociation (LD) subjects show the opposite: increased alpha and increased left-hemisphere beta (working memory engagement). HD subjects show less working memory activity, consistent with automaticity. LD subjects actively engage executive resources (Source: PMC12265433).

This means frontal decoupling is **one route** to hypnotic responding (the dissociative route), but not the only one. Absorptive subjects achieve equivalent behavioral responses through enhanced executive engagement rather than executive disengagement (Source: PMC12265433).

#### What This Looks Like in a Script

The **confusion method** (Source: scripts/confusion_method.txt) is the most direct script-level implementation of frontal decoupling. It explicitly addresses conscious and subconscious as separate entities, then drives a wedge between them:

> *"Your subconscious mind knows, and because your subconscious mind knows, your conscious mind does not need to know"* (Source: scripts/confusion_method.txt)

This logical chain presupposition creates a functional dissociation: knowing is attributed to one system, not-knowing to another. The frontal executive (the "conscious mind") is given permission to disengage — which neurologically maps to reduced DLPFC-to-DMN connectivity (Source: PMC12129887).

The paradoxes are the mechanism: *"You are aware of everything, and yet you are not aware"* (Source: scripts/confusion_method.txt). The conscious/analytical mind (Broca's area, BA45) cannot resolve a paradox — the statement is syntactically valid but semantically contradictory. The decreased BA45 connectivity observed during hypnosis (Source: PMC12178158) is what this script actively induces. The paradox forces a processing failure in the linguistic-analytical system, and the subject's attention shifts to the non-analytical processing mode that the "subconscious mind" label provides as an alternative.

The **Elman classic induction** (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt) achieves frontal decoupling through a different mechanism — the challenge-test cycle:

> *"Relax your eyes to the point where they just won't work... now test them to make sure they won't work"* (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt)

When the subject tries to open their eyes and finds they cannot, the executive monitoring system receives a prediction error: "I intended to open my eyes, but they didn't open." This incongruence between intention and outcome is exactly the agency-monitoring disruption that frontal decoupling produces (Source: PMC11852439). The Elman method doesn't argue or suggest — it creates a concrete behavioral experience of involuntariness, which the brain then generalizes. The subsequent generalization command — *"the same quality of relaxation in your eyes, let it flow through your entire body"* (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt) — extends the local decoupling (eyes) to a global decoupling.

### 4. Autonomic Reorganization

Hypnosis does not simply relax the body. It produces a paradoxical autonomic signature (Source: PMC12982806):

| Marker | Direction | Functional Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Tonic sympathetic activity (EDA) | **Increased** | Sustained engagement and alertness |
| Phasic sympathetic reactivity (SCR/min) | **Decreased** | Suppressed startle/stress surges |
| Parasympathetic recovery (HRV/RMSSD) | **Increased** | Enhanced vagal tone and recovery capacity |

This constitutes a shift from a threat autonomic profile (reactive, defensive) to a challenge autonomic profile (engaged, adaptive) (Source: PMC12982806). EDA was identified as the central hub linking stress responses and executive functions in network analysis (Source: PMC12982806).

**Classification power:** Machine learning classified hypnotic vs. control states from autonomic markers alone: AUC = 0.91, 90% cross-validated accuracy. Key predictors: change in perceived stress, post-intervention VAS stress, change in RMSSD, change in EDA (Source: PMC12982806).

**Endocrinological dimension:** Hypnosis reduces cortisol and elevates oxytocin (Source: PMC11852439). The "calm contact" technique — imagined social connection — leverages oxytocin release as a neurochemical mechanism (Source: PMC11852439).

**Parasympathetic dominance confirmed across populations:** Pregnant women receiving group hypnotherapy showed sustained LF/HF ratio increase (increased parasympathetic activation, p<0.001) that persisted through 38 weeks gestation, while controls returned to baseline (Source: PMC12611927). COPD patients receiving dyspnoea-oriented hypnosis maintained improvement at 6 months; controls returned to baseline (Source: PMC12862456).

**Descending pain modulation:** Hypnotic suggestions can alter nociceptive flexion reflex (NFR) amplitude at the spinal level, demonstrating activation of descending modulatory mechanisms (Source: PMC12599604, PMC12767259). Hypnosis modulates endogenous opioids, serotonin, and norepinephrine in the descending pain pathway (Source: PMC12767259).

#### What This Looks Like in a Script

The **body conditioning** script (Source: scripts/body_conditioning.txt) systematically drives autonomic reorganization through exhaustive body scanning. The instruction *"Turn all those muscles loose"* (not "relax" but "turn loose") with the recurring metaphor *"like loose rubber bands"* creates a kinesthetic prediction of parasympathetic activation — the body is being told to shift from tonic muscle engagement (sympathetic) to release (parasympathetic):

> *"Let relaxation move into your scalp... your forehead... your eyes..."* (Source: scripts/body_conditioning.txt)

The personification of relaxation as an entity "moving into" body regions maps to the autonomic finding: relaxation is not something you do, it is something that propagates. This mirrors the observed parasympathetic increase spreading through the autonomic nervous system during hypnosis (Source: PMC12982806).

The **"white mist" exhale visualization** in the same script — exhaling tension as visible mist — creates a predictive coding frame where each breath cycle is associated with measurable tension release. The EDA tonic increase (sustained alertness) alongside phasic decrease (suppressed stress surges) may correspond to the script's simultaneous maintenance of engagement ("Listen carefully") and release of tension ("let it go").

The **deep relaxation method** (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt) uses the **puppet metaphor** as a dramatic autonomic trigger:

> *"Feel like a puppet that has strings attached... I cut all of your puppet strings at once"* (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt)

This is a single-moment autonomic collapse instruction — a sudden shift from tonic engagement (strings maintaining posture) to complete parasympathetic release (all strings cut simultaneously). The metaphor's power lies in its compression: a complex autonomic transition is condensed into a single concrete image that the brain can execute as a unified prediction.

The **Elman rapid induction** (Source: scripts/elman_rapid_induction.txt) uses the triple release pattern: *"Let go of every muscle. Let go of every nerve. Let go of every fibre..."* — an exhaustive enumeration that creates progressively finer-grained parasympathetic activation by addressing successively smaller units of the body. The escalating specificity (muscle → nerve → fiber) corresponds to progressively deeper autonomic penetration.

### 5. The Language-as-Precision-Instrument Mechanism

Hypnotic language operates differently from normal speech at multiple levels, and the script corpus reveals the specific linguistic tools:

#### 5.1 The Power of the Frame

The word "hypnosis" alone shifts neural responses and subjective depth by 3.41 points on a 0-10 scale — even when applied to white noise (Source: PMC12595402). White noise labeled "hypnosis" produced 40.9% hot flash reduction vs. 53.4% for real hypnosis — a contentless stimulus carrying only the label achieved 77% of the active treatment's effect (Source: PMC12606380). Critically, EEG features could NOT distinguish conventional from unconventional inductions; labeling changed subjective experience but not measurable brain states (Source: PMC12595402).

**Script implementation:** Every script in the corpus begins with a frame-setting operation. The Elman classic induction opens with an explicit consent frame: a pre-talk establishing that hypnosis will occur (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt). The misdirection method (Source: scripts/misdirection_method.txt) deliberately conceals the frame — the subject believes they are doing "imagination tests" while the actual induction is buried inside what appears to be a visualization exercise. The eye roll ("look at the full moon → roll your eyeballs back") becomes the hidden induction trigger. This script exploits frame-primacy by withholding the frame until the moment of induction, when the realization "this IS hypnosis" coincides with the physiological trigger.

#### 5.2 The Verb "Let" — The Master Operator

Across the entire script corpus, "let" is the single most important verb (Source: corpus-wide analysis of scripts/). It appears in virtually every script and serves multiple simultaneous functions:

- **Permission grant:** "let yourself relax" — activates participation without triggering evaluative resistance (Source: PMC12767259)
- **Release command:** "let go" — direct instruction to disengage frontal monitoring
- **Passive causation:** "let your eyes close" — creates an involuntariness frame where closure "happens to" the subject
- **Agency transfer:** "let it happen" — shifts the locus of control from the subject's executive system to the suggestion

The verb "let" is the linguistic equivalent of frontal decoupling: it instructs the executive system to release control rather than execute control. In predictive coding terms, "let" reduces the precision weighting of volitional predictions, allowing suggestion-generated predictions to dominate (Source: PMC13024316, PMC12358409).

The **handshake method** (Source: scripts/handshake_method.txt) compresses the full "let" sequence into three sentences: *"Let it happen. Want it to happen. Feel it happening now."* This is a three-beat acceleration from permission → volition → presupposed completion — the subject is moved from executive disengagement ("let") through intentional alignment ("want") to experiential certainty ("feel it happening") in three seconds.

#### 5.3 Permissive vs. Authoritative Language Spectrum

The corpus reveals a clear spectrum from authoritative to permissive language:

**Authoritative pole** (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt, scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt, scripts/stiff_arm_induction.txt):
- Imperatives: "Close your eyes," "Sleep now," "Don't take your eyes from mine"
- Absolute presuppositions: "There is nothing in this world that can keep you from entering a very deep and pleasant state" (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt)
- Massive repetition: "Closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing" — 8x in one sentence (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt)

**Permissive pole** (Source: scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt, scripts/awareness_induction.txt, scripts/favorite_place.txt):
- Hedged suggestions: "you might notice," "perhaps," "I wonder if," "it may be that" (Source: scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt)
- Strategic ignorance: "I'm not sure where your favorite place is — I know that you know" (Source: scripts/favorite_place.txt) — forces the client to generate content, increasing engagement
- Multi-option offers: "Perhaps you can feel it in your chest... or maybe in your temples" (Source: scripts/awareness_induction.txt)

**Neurological mapping:** Authoritative language likely engages the dissociative route (HD subjects, frontal decoupling, alpha decrease) — direct commands suppress executive engagement. Permissive language likely engages the absorptive route (LD subjects, enhanced executive engagement, alpha increase) — hedged suggestions invite participation rather than submission. The dual-route architecture (Source: PMC12265433) predicts that authoritative and permissive scripts should produce different EEG signatures in the same subject, yet potentially equivalent behavioral outcomes.

#### 5.4 Embedded Commands and Indirect Speech

Ericksonian technique embeds suggestions within conversational narratives, bypassing conscious resistance through indirect speech acts (Source: PMC13024316, PMC12767259). The script corpus reveals specific patterns:

- **Pacing-leading:** "As you [observable X], you [suggested Y]" — found in every script. Example: "as you listen to my voice, you can begin to relax" links an already-occurring behavior to a desired one (Source: scripts/nlp_secrets_induction.txt)
- **Presuppositions:** "When you relax even further..." presupposes ongoing relaxation (Source: scripts/progressive_relaxation.txt). "Before you let go completely and go into a deep hypnotic state" presupposes that complete letting-go is imminent (Source: scripts/favorite_place.txt)
- **Double binds:** "Whether you notice it now or in a moment" — the question is not IF but WHEN (Source: corpus-wide pattern)
- **Negation for suggestion:** "There is no need to fight any unwanted negative thoughts" — the sentence introduces the very concept it negates, priming the subject to think about (and then release) negative thoughts (Source: corpus-wide pattern)

#### 5.5 Metaphor as Neural Pathway

Metaphorical suggestions create novel cognitive mappings that restructure neural processing (Source: PMC12767259). The corpus contains systematic metaphorical patterns:

**Release metaphors:**
- "Like loose rubber bands" (Source: scripts/body_conditioning.txt) — muscle tension to elasticity
- "Just as loose and limp as a wet dish cloth" (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt) — body to inanimate object
- "Like a puppet that has strings attached... I cut all your puppet strings at once" (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt) — controlled-to-uncontrolled transition

**Descent/deepening metaphors:**
- Hammock on a warm day (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt) — combining spatial descent with safety
- Elevator descending floor by floor (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt) — countdown + spatial descent
- Staircase descent into a corridor with doors (Source: scripts/theta-meditation-script.txt) — combining depth with exploration

**Transformation metaphors:**
- Gas-filled balloon for lightness (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt)
- Steel bar for rigidity (Source: scripts/stiff_arm_induction.txt)
- White mist for exhaled tension (Source: scripts/body_conditioning.txt)

Each metaphor works through predictive coding: it provides the brain with a concrete physical-world model whose predictions override the current sensory state. "Loose rubber bands" is not just a description — it is a prediction that the brain can execute, generating the kinesthetic experience of looseness (Source: PMC12994519 — mental imagery activates the same sensory processing regions as actual experience).

#### 5.6 Left-Hemisphere Suppression

Broca's area connectivity decreases during hypnosis, correlating with a shift toward right-hemisphere holistic processing (Source: PMC12178158). BA45 (left Broca's homolog) showed decreased connectivity with all other measured PFC regions, while BA46 (DLPFC) showed increased interhemispheric connectivity (Source: PMC12178158).

**Script implementation:** The repetitive, rhythmic structures ubiquitous in hypnotic scripts — "deeper and deeper and deeper," "every nerve, every muscle, every fiber" (Source: scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt, scripts/elman_rapid_induction.txt) — may specifically target left-hemisphere suppression. Repetition reduces the analytical processing load (Broca's area has less to parse) while the rhythm engages right-hemisphere pattern-processing. The eightfold "closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing, closing" in the direct gaze method (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt) is an extreme example — the analytical language system effectively shuts down when confronted with content-free repetition, while the prosodic/rhythmic processing (right hemisphere) is maximally engaged.

### 6. Suggestibility: Architecture, Not Gate

The traditional model (10-15% high, 10-15% low, majority moderate) is increasingly inadequate:

- Personalized suggestions produce effects **independent of measured hypnotizability** (Source: PMC12982806)
- 85-90% of the general population can achieve clinically relevant effects with appropriate personalization (Source: PMC12982806)
- Hypnotizability is **modifiable**: transcranial stimulation of L-DLPFC transiently enhances hypnotizability by strengthening DLPFC-dACC connectivity (Source: PMC12982806, PMC13024316, PMC12652343)
- Labeling and expectancy are stronger predictors of response than trait suggestibility scores (Source: PMC12595402)
- Higher HIP scores correspond to increased fMRI connectivity between left DLPFC and dorsal ACC (Source: PMC12775527)
- FND patients respond at 87% improvement rates despite normal suggestibility scores (Source: PMC12652343) — their aberrant DMN-SaN architecture makes them structurally more responsive to the network reorganization hypnosis produces (Source: PMC12258340)
- Phenomenological control capacity — the ability to align perceptual experiences with intentions — correlates weakly with positive schizotypy but is a distinct construct from traditional suggestibility (Source: PMC12094076)

**Scalability evidence:** An app-delivered self-hypnosis study of N=84,395 users showed consistent stress reduction (Cohen's d = -0.71 to -0.78 across first 10 sessions). Only 10 complaints of worsening across 84,395 users — extremely safe. Hypnotizability moderated effects but did not gate them (Source: PMC12775527).

**Children as natural hypnotic subjects:** Children's natural absorption and imagination capacity makes them excellent responders. Techniques must be developmentally tailored — guided imagery, storytelling, self-hypnosis (Source: PMC12767259). Connectivity changes persist beyond the hypnotic state, supporting lasting benefit (Source: PMC12767259).

## II. THE BRIDGE: SCRIPT TECHNIQUE → NEURAL MECHANISM

This section maps every major script-level technique to its neural substrate, creating a bidirectional reference between "what the hypnotist says" and "what the brain does."

### Bridge 1: Progressive Relaxation → Parasympathetic Cascade + DMN Suppression

**Script technique:** Top-to-toe body scan naming individual muscle groups (Source: scripts/progressive_relaxation.txt, scripts/body_conditioning.txt, scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** Each named body region redirects SaN attention to interoceptive signals, suppressing DMN self-referential processing (Source: PMC13024316). The progressive structure creates a sustained parasympathetic cascade: tonic EDA increases (maintained engagement with the task) while phasic sympathetic reactivity decreases (no surprise/threat signals) (Source: PMC12982806). The kinesthetic focus explains why these scripts are almost entirely body-focused with minimal visualization or metaphor (Source: scripts/progressive_relaxation.txt) — the sensory channel is kinesthetic because the target system is autonomic.

**The "more you physically relax, the more you can mentally relax" bridge** found in the progressive relaxation script (Source: scripts/progressive_relaxation.txt) is a pacing-leading statement that linguistically connects the parasympathetic cascade (physical relaxation) to DMN suppression (mental relaxation). This is not just rhetoric — the autonomic-cognitive coupling documented in network analysis (EDA as central hub, Source: PMC12982806) means physical relaxation literally drives the neural conditions for mental quieting.

### Bridge 2: Countdown/Staircase Deepeners → Theta Entrainment + Thalamocortical Modulation

**Script technique:** Numbered countdowns from 10→1 or 20→1, staircase descent visualizations, elevator descents (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt — 20-count; scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt — dual countdown + elevator; scripts/theta-meditation-script.txt — 10-step staircase).

**Neural mechanism:** Rhythmic counting at ~1 count per 3-5 seconds maps to theta frequency (4-8 Hz). Increased theta power — particularly left parietal, occipital, and frontal regions — is among the most consistent EEG markers of hypnosis (Source: PMC11852439, PMC12265433, PMC12358409). Post-hypnosis theta-band entropy decreases significantly (Source: PMC12011983), indicating more ordered oscillatory patterns. Theta oscillations in the thalamocortical system are generated when thalamocortical neurons become hyperpolarized — every thalamocortical neuron can become a delta/theta generator (Source: PMC12265433). The rhythmic descent structure may directly entrain these thalamocortical circuits.

**Spatial metaphor component:** The staircase, elevator, and "going deeper" metaphors all encode spatial descent, which maps to the phenomenological experience of increasing hypnotic depth. The theta-meditation-script (Source: scripts/theta-meditation-script.txt) uses a 10-step staircase leading to a corridor with doors — the spatial navigation metaphor may engage the hippocampal theta rhythm specifically, since the hippocampus generates theta during spatial navigation and memory processing.

### Bridge 3: The Challenge-Collapse Pattern → Agency Disruption + Frontal Decoupling

**Script technique:** Create rigidity → prove it through challenge → release for dramatic deepening. Found in eye catalepsy (Source: scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt), arm rigidity (Source: scripts/stiff_arm_induction.txt), hand lock (Source: scripts/clasp_induction.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** The challenge moment ("try to open your eyes and find that you cannot") creates a prediction error in the agency-monitoring system: the intention to act is generated but the predicted movement does not occur (Source: PMC11852439, PMC12129887). This is precisely the "disruption in ECN functional integration linked to altered agency" documented in neuroimaging (Source: PMC11852439). The subsequent "collapse" (releasing the rigidity into relaxation) deepens the decoupling — the executive system, having just failed to execute an intended action, releases its grip on motor control. The depth of trance after a successful challenge is consistently reported as greater than before it (clinical observation across scripts), which neurologically corresponds to increased frontal decoupling following an agency-prediction error.

**The compliance escalation ladder** in the clasp induction (Source: scripts/clasp_induction.txt) — extend hands → involuntary movement → can't unclasp → release — is a graduated frontal decoupling protocol. Each step commits the subject to deeper involuntariness, progressively weakening the executive system's prediction that it controls the body.

### Bridge 4: Confusion/Paradox → Left-Hemisphere Suppression + Processing Mode Shift

**Script technique:** Paradoxical statements, conscious/subconscious splitting, logical contradiction (Source: scripts/confusion_method.txt, scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** Paradoxes ("You are aware of everything, and yet you are not aware" — Source: scripts/confusion_method.txt) create processing failures in the linguistic-analytical system (Broca's area, BA45). The decreased BA45 connectivity observed in fNIRS during hypnosis (Source: PMC12178158) is what confusion techniques actively produce. When the left-hemisphere analytical system cannot resolve a contradiction, attention shifts to right-hemisphere holistic processing — the same interhemispheric shift that increased bilateral DLPFC (BA46) connectivity reflects (Source: PMC12178158).

The **seven_plus_or_minus_two** script's explicit naming of the psychological mechanism (Source: scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt) is itself a confusion technique: processing an abstract cognitive concept WHILE experiencing its effects creates a meta-cognitive overload that accelerates the processing mode shift. The subject is simultaneously the experiment and the experimenter — a paradox that the analytical system cannot maintain.

### Bridge 5: Ideomotor Suggestions → Predictive Coding Override + Involuntariness

**Script technique:** Suggestions for involuntary movement — arm levitation, hand magnetism, eye catalepsy (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt, scripts/clasp_induction.txt, scripts/handshake_method.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** Ideomotor suggestions work through predictive coding (Source: PMC13024316): the suggestion generates a motor prediction (arm will rise) that is sufficiently precise to generate actual motor output. The subject experiences this as involuntary because the prediction bypasses metacognitive monitoring — it was generated by the suggestion, not by the subject's intention system (Source: PMC12358409 — "inaccurate metacognition of intentions: voluntary actions experienced as involuntary").

The arm levitation method's **graduated sensory progression** (tingling → spreading → lightness → movement → lifting → floating; Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt) is a prediction-precision escalation. Each step makes the prediction slightly more precise, progressively narrowing the prediction error until the motor system executes the predicted movement. The subject doesn't suddenly "decide" to lift their arm — the prediction becomes so precise that it would require active inhibition NOT to lift it.

### Bridge 6: Nature/Safe Place Visualization → DMN Repurposing + Oxytocin Release

**Script technique:** Detailed nature scenes (forest, stream, meadow), "favorite place" or "safe place" visualization (Source: scripts/forest_and_stream.txt, scripts/favorite_place.txt, scripts/theta-meditation-script.txt, scripts/deep_relaxation_method.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** The DMN normally generates self-referential narratives. Nature visualization repurposes the DMN's imagery-generation capacity toward non-self-referential content — the subject simulates a scene rather than ruminating (Source: PMC13024316 — DMN hyperactivity linked to rumination; reduction enables flexible processing). The "calm contact" technique documented in research — imagined social connection triggering oxytocin release (Source: PMC11852439) — explains why "safe place" visualizations that include emotional warmth produce deeper autonomic effects than pure nature scenes.

The **forest_and_stream** script (Source: scripts/forest_and_stream.txt) demonstrates a unique structural technique: it transitions from second-person hypnotic language ("you are walking through a forest...") to first-person affirmations ("I am calm, secure, and relaxed..."). This transition represents a shift from externally-generated DMN content (the hypnotist's scene) to internally-generated DMN content (the subject's own self-statements) — a gradual return of DMN function, but now configured for positive self-reference rather than rumination.

### Bridge 7: Rapid Inductions → Pattern Interrupt + Startle-to-Compliance Pathway

**Script technique:** "Sleep now!" commands, physical interrupts, ultra-brief inductions (Source: scripts/instantaneous_rapid_induction.txt, scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt, scripts/handshake_method.txt).

**Neural mechanism:** Rapid inductions exploit the startle-to-compliance pathway. The instantaneous rapid induction (Source: scripts/instantaneous_rapid_induction.txt) positions the subject with feet close together (creating physical instability), then delivers the snap command "Sleep now." The physical instability creates a momentary salience spike (SaN activation) as the brain detects a balance threat. The "Sleep now" command arrives at the peak of this salience spike, hijacking the SaN's heightened attention and redirecting it from the balance threat to the suggestion (Source: PMC13024316 — SaN switching between DMN and ECN; the snap command forces a switch to ECN-coupled suggestion processing).

The **direct gaze method's** eightfold repetition of "closing" (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt) works through a different mechanism — it creates an auditory rhythm so dominant that the analytical system (BA45) saturates and disengages (Source: PMC12178158 — decreased BA45 connectivity). The "Heavy, droopy, drowsy and sleepy" alliterative chain concentrates on a single phonemic pattern (/d/ and /i/ sounds), further reducing linguistic processing diversity and promoting left-hemisphere suppression.

## III. ANOMALIES & UNEXPLAINED PHENOMENA

### A-1. The Sleep-Anaesthesia Dissociation
Ablating 80% of parafacial zone GABAergic (PZGABA) neurons in mice produced anaesthetic resistance but zero insomnia — directly falsifying the hypothesis that anaesthetics co-opt natural sleep circuitry. Behavioral resistance occurred without corresponding EEG changes: the brain "looked" anaesthetized while the animal was behaviorally awake (Source: PMC12106870). PZGABA neurons are necessary for volatile anaesthetic sensitivity but not for normal sleep maintenance. Consciousness may operate at levels invisible to surface electrophysiology.

**Thalamic specificity:** CaV3.1 T-type voltage-gated calcium channels play region-specific roles in anesthetic hypnosis. Knocking down CaV3.1 in midline/intralaminar (MIT) thalamus vs. ventrobasal (VB) thalamus produces distinct effects on loss of righting and withdrawal reflexes (Source: PMC12123693). This regional specificity suggests that different components of "hypnotic unconsciousness" are mediated by different thalamic circuits.

### A-2. The Reflex Paradox
Hypnosis simultaneously reduces subjective pain perception while the nociceptive flexion reflex (NFR) shows complex modulation — relaxation hypnosis without specific pain suggestions shows WEAKER NFR effects than VR or VR-hypnosis combinations (Source: PMC12599604). The content of the suggestion matters: generic relaxation does not activate descending pain modulatory pathways as effectively as direct hypoalgesic suggestions. Yet hypnosis activates descending modulatory mechanisms at the spinal level (Source: PMC12599604, PMC12767259), and the reflex-perception dissociation remains unexplained by current models.

### A-3. The Sham Problem
White noise labeled "hypnosis" produced 40.9% hot flash reduction vs. 53.4% for real hypnosis (Source: PMC12606380). A contentless stimulus carrying only the label achieved 77% of the active treatment's effect. But EEG could NOT distinguish conventional from unconventional inductions — the labeling changed subjective experience but not measurable brain states (Source: PMC12595402). This creates a paradox: the frame produces real clinical effects without detectable neurophysiological correlates.

### A-4. The Gamma Direction Contradiction
HD subjects show increased right-hemisphere prefrontal gamma during hypnosis (consistent with imagery-like processing, functional equivalence between imagery and perception). LD subjects show the opposite pattern (Source: PMC12265433). Multiple studies report opposite directions of gamma power change. This is not a quantitative discrepancy — it is a directional inversion that reflects the dual-route architecture: different subjects reach the same behavioral destination through neurologically opposite paths.

### A-5. Memory Enhancement AND Distortion Simultaneously
Hypnosis both improves memory recall and creates false memories via source misattribution and imagery inflation. Hypnotic regression increases confidence in memory accuracy even for false memories (Source: PMC11832514). Post-hypnotic amnesia involves two distinct mechanisms: recall amnesia (blocking retrieval) and source amnesia (losing the contextual tag of when/where a memory was encoded) — both mediated by DLPFC-hippocampal connectivity disruption (Source: PMC11832514, PMC12129887).

### A-6. FND Patients Respond Despite Normal Suggestibility
Patients with FND show 87% improvement rates with hypnosis but are NOT more suggestible than healthy controls (Source: PMC12652343). Their aberrant DMN-salience network architecture — hyperconnectivity across fronto-parietal, sensorimotor, executive, and DMN networks (Source: PMC12258340) — may make them structurally pre-configured for the network reorganization hypnosis produces. Thalamocortical dysrhythmia (abnormal theta and beta oscillations) is a shared neural signature across FND and chronic pain (Source: PMC12258340) — and hypnosis may correct this dysrhythmia directly.

### A-7. The Acute vs. Chronic Pain Cliff
Meta-analysis shows hypnosis produces medium, significant effects on acute pain (0.54 SD decrease, p = 0.0024) and significant opioid reduction (1.5 SD lower, p = 0.03), but NO significant effect on chronic pain (Hedges' g = 0.07, p = 0.518) (Source: PMC12250368). This cliff is unexplained: if hypnosis modulates the same pain processing circuitry in both conditions, why does acute pain respond and chronic pain does not? One hypothesis: chronic pain involves thalamocortical dysrhythmia (Source: PMC12258340) that is too deeply entrenched for a single-session override. Another: chronic pain's central sensitization has restructured the predictive model to a degree that requires sustained re-training rather than acute override.

### A-8. The Hidden Observer Persists
Hilgard's "hidden observer" — a dissociated monitoring consciousness that persists during hypnosis — has never been explained or disproven (Source: PMC12767259). It maps onto the PMC12106870 finding of behavioral-EEG incongruence: awareness may be layered, with some layers inaccessible to standard measurement.

## IV. MISSED CONNECTIONS & CROSS-DOMAIN BRIDGES

### M-1. Ketamine as Chemical Hypnosis — And the Plasticity Window Hypothesis

Ketamine therapy produces the same frontal decoupling, dissociative phenomenology, and theta-band amplification as hypnotic trance (Source: PMC12497829). But the ketamine literature reveals the critical insight: the acute dissociative experience is **neither necessary nor sufficient** for therapeutic success. The therapeutic mechanism is the BDNF-mediated neuroplasticity window that opens in the DAYS following administration, not during dissociation (Source: PMC12497829). Psychotherapy during peak dissociation was compared to "planting seeds in frozen ground" (Source: PMC12497829).

**Implication for hypnosis:** The hypnotic state may similarly be preparatory rather than directly therapeutic. The healing happens in the reorganization phase, not the trance. The durability data supports this: COPD patients maintained improvement at 6 months only when self-hypnosis training was included (Source: PMC12862456); pregnant women maintained parasympathetic gains only when continuing practice (Source: PMC12611927). The trance opens the window; the sustained practice (self-hypnosis, post-session integration) does the remodeling.

**Script implication:** The **emergence/reorientation** phase of hypnotic scripts may be more therapeutically important than the trance itself. Scripts that include post-hypnotic suggestions and self-hypnosis training (Source: scripts/nlp_self_hypnosis_relaxation.txt — "Bringing everything that you've liked about this back with you"; Source: scripts/forest_and_stream.txt — first-person affirmation installation) may produce more durable effects than scripts that simply "count up" from trance.

### M-2. Meditation and Hypnosis — Same Networks, Opposite Metacognition

Buddhist meditation and hypnosis converge on identical network signatures: DMN deactivation, enhanced absorption, altered self-referential processing (Source: PMC12358409, PMC13024316). Samatha meditation increases frontal midline theta (generated by ACC and prefrontal regions); vipassana increases parieto-occipital gamma and decreases prefrontal gamma (Source: PMC12358409). Both share DMN modulation.

**The critical difference:** Hypnosis involves "inaccurate metacognition of intentions" — voluntary actions experienced as involuntary. Meditation cultivates "enhanced metacognitive access" — the opposite direction (Source: PMC12358409). They suppress the same narrative self but through opposite metacognitive mechanisms.

**Mindful hypnotherapy evidence:** Combining hypnotic induction with mindfulness suggestions produces robust effects: Hedges' g = 0.61 for psychological distress, 0.75 for stress, and 1.38 for mindfulness acquisition (Source: PMC12837898). The integration may be synergistic: hypnosis provides the network reconfiguration, mindfulness provides the metacognitive framework for sustaining it.

**Script convergence:** The **awareness induction** (Source: scripts/awareness_induction.txt) is the most meditation-like script in the corpus — it uses present-moment body awareness, breathing observation without modification, and multi-sensory noticing, all hallmarks of mindfulness practice. The fact that this script produces hypnotic trance through mindfulness techniques supports the "same networks, different entry points" thesis (Source: PMC12358409, PMC13024316).

### M-3. The FND-Hypnosis-Placebo Triangle

FND, hypnosis, and placebo share overlapping neural substrates: ACC, insula, prefrontal cortex, amygdala, nucleus accumbens, hippocampus, brainstem (Source: PMC12652343, PMC12258340). The "placebo network" and the "hypnosis network" and the "FND dysfunction network" are the same network in different states.

**The "split-screen" technique:** FND patients can learn to consciously elicit and suppress symptoms through suggestion/self-hypnosis, gaining voluntary control over symptoms that were previously involuntary (Source: PMC12652343). This directly demonstrates that hypnotic suggestion can reverse the predictive coding error that produces FND symptoms.

**Thalamocortical dysrhythmia as unifying mechanism:** Both FND and chronic pain show elevated low-frequency (theta/delta) activity in fronto-limbic and DMN regions (Source: PMC12258340). Hypnosis modulates theta activity (Source: PMC12011983, PMC12265433, PMC11852439). The connection is direct but unexploited: hypnosis may specifically target the thalamocortical dysrhythmia underlying both FND and chronic pain.

### M-4. Neuromodulation Can Bootstrap Hypnotizability

TMS to L-DLPFC enhances hypnotizability by strengthening the DLPFC-dACC circuit (Source: PMC12982806, PMC13024316, PMC12652343). This creates a bootstrapping possibility: use neuromodulation to make someone hypnotizable enough to benefit from hypnosis, then use hypnosis to maintain the network configuration. Barely explored.

### M-5. Computational Linguistic Analysis — The Largest Gap, Now Partially Filled

Across 50 papers, not one performs computational linguistic analysis of hypnotic scripts (Source: corpus-wide observation). However, the script corpus now enables this analysis. Pre-existing analysis tools (`analyze_final.py`, `analyze_corpus_v2.py` in Source: scripts/) classify scripts by "you-density," extract Milton Model patterns, measure sensory channel distribution, and identify trance grammar features across the 35 pure-script subset.

**Key computational findings from corpus analysis** (Source: scripts/analyze_final.py, scripts/analyze_corpus_v2.py):
- **Kinesthetic language dominates** the sensory channel distribution (heavy, warm, tingling, comfortable, tension, soft, smooth), followed by visual, then auditory
- **Master verb clusters:** Release (let go/release/drop/sink/drift/float/melt), Relaxation (relax/rest/calm/ease/settle/soften), Perception (notice/feel/sense/become aware), Imagination (imagine/picture/visualize/see), Deepening (deepen/deeper/sink/drift)
- **Structural pattern:** ~70% of scripts follow induction → deepening → suggestion → emergence
- **Repetitive intensifiers:** "deeper and deeper" is the most common phrase across the corpus; triple patterns ("every nerve, every muscle, every fiber") appear in nearly every script
- **Nominalization density:** Extremely high — process words turned into nouns (relaxation, awareness, comfort, sensation, experience) are the backbone of hypnotic grammar

### M-6. Digital Delivery and Population Scale

App-delivered self-hypnosis (Reveri, developed with Spiegel) at N=84,395 users showed consistent, significant stress reduction with minimal adverse effects (Source: PMC12775527). Group hypnotherapy is effective for sustained autonomic and psychological benefits (Source: PMC12611927). SmartACT integration (ACT + hypnosis + guided imagery) outperformed single-modality approaches for both motor and psychological outcomes (Source: PMC12562448). Self-administered hypnosis was effective for vasomotor symptoms (Source: PMC12606380). The field's dependence on individual in-person delivery is a choice, not a constraint.

### M-7. The Evolutionary Thread

Tonic immobility — "animal hypnosis" — is an ancestral survival strategy found across phylogenetic spectrum, modulated by brainstem, limbic system, striatum, and cortex (Source: PMC12857180, PMC12714132). The shared evolutionary origins of tonic immobility and human hypnotic-like states suggest that hypnosis co-opts a conserved neural architecture for immobility and reduced reactivity. Octopus tonic immobility produces CNS-PNS dissociation analogous to the frontal decoupling in human hypnosis (Source: PMC12714132).

## V. META-PATTERNS

### The Precision-Absorption Continuum

Every mechanism above maps onto a single axis: **how precisely the brain's predictive model is captured by the suggestion** (Source: PMC13024316, PMC12994519). Low precision (generic relaxation script) = relaxation response + modest autonomic shift. Medium precision (personalized progressive relaxation) = behavioral compliance + autonomic reorganization. High precision (vivid imagery with ideomotor suggestion) = genuine perceptual alteration + involuntary motor response.

The mechanism is always predictive coding override; what changes is the resolution. The scripts confirm this: the **progressive relaxation** script (Source: scripts/progressive_relaxation.txt) — low precision, naming body parts without vivid imagery — produces physical relaxation. The **arm levitation** script (Source: scripts/arm_levitation_method.txt) — high precision, numbered sequence with graduated sensory detail and concrete metaphor — produces involuntary movement.

### The Dual-Route Architecture

Two independent neural pathways to hypnotic responding (Source: PMC12265433):

1. **Dissociative route**: Frontal decoupling → reduced agency monitoring → involuntariness (HD subjects, alpha decrease, right-hemisphere gamma increase, less working memory activity). **Script affinity:** Authoritative inductions, challenge-tests, paradoxical suggestions, "Sleep now" commands (Source: scripts/direct_gaze_method.txt, scripts/elman_classic_induction.txt, scripts/confusion_method.txt)

2. **Absorptive route**: Enhanced executive engagement → focused attention → suggestion compliance (LD subjects, alpha increase, left-hemisphere beta increase, active working memory). **Script affinity:** Permissive inductions, guided imagery, client-directed visualization, self-hypnosis (Source: scripts/awareness_induction.txt, scripts/favorite_place.txt, scripts/seven_plus_or_minus_two.txt)

Both routes converge on the same behavioral output through different neural mechanisms. The field has been treating these as one phenomenon. The script corpus confirms the duality: authoritative and permissive scripts represent distinct traditions that may be targeting different neural populations.

### The Frame > Content Principle

Across studies AND scripts, contextual framing consistently outweighs specific technique content:

- The label "hypnosis" matters more than the induction procedure (Source: PMC12595402)
- White noise + label achieves 77% of active treatment's effect (Source: PMC12606380)
- Personalization matters more than standardized protocol (Source: PMC12982806)
- Expectancy matters more than suggestibility score (Source: PMC12595402)
- Patient openness, readiness, attitude, and expectations more important than delivery method (Source: PMC12250368)
- The misdirection method (Source: scripts/misdirection_method.txt) works by concealing the frame until the induction moment, demonstrating that frame-timing — not just frame-content — is a variable

This suggests hypnosis is fundamentally a **frame-setting operation** that reconfigures the brain's processing context. The scripts are the delivery vehicle, not the active ingredient. But the scripts shape the precision of the reconfiguration — which is why the mismatch between "frame sufficient" and "script precision matters" is not a contradiction but a spectrum.

### The Durability Principle

Across clinical studies, hypnosis effects are durable ONLY when accompanied by self-hypnosis training or structured reorientation:
- COPD patients: improvement persisted at 6 months only in hypnosis group that received self-hypnosis training (Source: PMC12862456)
- Pregnant women: sustained improvement only with continued practice (Source: PMC12611927)
- Stressed medical students: acute effects strong but no long-term follow-up (Source: PMC12982806)
- Mindful hypnotherapy: robust effects at g = 0.61-1.38, with ongoing practice component (Source: PMC12837898)

This maps to the neuroplasticity window hypothesis (M-1): the trance opens a window; sustained practice remodels the circuitry. Scripts that include post-hypnotic anchoring (Source: scripts/nlp_self_hypnosis_relaxation.txt — "a post-hypnotic suggestion for faster future induction") and self-hypnosis instruction (Source: scripts/theta-meditation-script.txt — full practitioner training on self-use) are implicitly implementing this principle.

## VII. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR V3

1. **The EEG-behavior gap:** Why does labeling change clinical outcomes (Source: PMC12606380) but NOT EEG signatures (Source: PMC12595402)? Where does the frame-effect live neurologically if not in measurable oscillations?

2. **The chronic pain cliff:** What makes acute pain responsive (d = 0.54) and chronic pain unresponsive (g = 0.07) to hypnosis (Source: PMC12250368)? Is thalamocortical dysrhythmia (Source: PMC12258340) the barrier?

3. **Optimal script-to-subject matching:** Can the dual-route architecture (Source: PMC12265433) be used to prescribe authoritative scripts for HD subjects and permissive scripts for LD subjects? No study has tested this.

4. **The emergence phase:** Is the reorientation/emergence phase the actual therapeutic window (M-1), and if so, how should scripts be redesigned to optimize it?

5. **Computational language analysis validation:** Do the corpus-level linguistic features (kinesthetic dominance, "let" verb frequency, nominalization density, repetitive intensifiers) predict clinical efficacy when tested prospectively? (Source: Hypothesis H5 in hypotheses.md)

6. **Sex differences:** Females show increased sensitivity to neuroactive steroid-induced hypnosis (Source: PMC12185358, PMC12293945) and faster consciousness recovery from anesthesia (Source: PMC12368020). Do sex-dependent effects extend to psychological hypnosis, and should scripts be tailored accordingly?

7. **The endogenous neurochemistry bridge:** Hypnosis modulates cortisol, oxytocin, endogenous opioids, serotonin, and norepinephrine (Source: PMC11852439, PMC12767259). How do specific script elements map to specific neurochemical effects?
