# Holographic Knowledge Mesh: Hypnosis & NLP

This directory contains an evolving, "holographic" understanding of Hypnosis and Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP).

## The Hologram Principle
Our understanding is not a flat list of facts. It is a multi-dimensional mesh. Every new paper we ingest adds clarity, detail, and resolution to the overall picture. We look for:
1. Intersections between cognitive science, neuroscience, and clinical NLP.
2. Unexplained anomalies and footnotes that hint at deeper truths.
3. Testable hypotheses generated from the synthesis of multiple papers.

## Files
- `mesh.md`: The core "hologram". A heavily cross-referenced, evolving document mapping all concepts, mechanisms, and theories.
- `hypotheses.md`: A list of testable, novel hypotheses derived from the mesh.
- `sources.md`: A bibliography of all ingested papers.
- `engine.sh`: The automated Claude Code ingest loop.
- `updates/`: Daily logs of what was added and how the hologram changed.
