Good morning,

Please see the highlighted response regarding your inquiry:


  1.  Is the NYC DOT currently accepting applications for new sightseeing bus stop permits?- Yes; Owner/Operator must submit proof of Sightseeing Bus License. For information on obtaining a Sightseeing Bus License please refer to The New York City Department of Consumer and Worker Protection<https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/businesses/license-checklist-sightseeing-bus.page> website.
  2.  Is there a waiting list or a moratorium on new stops in Manhattan?- No, there is no waiting list or moratorium in place.
  3.  Can new operators apply for stops at existing locations (shared use), or must we identify entirely new locations?- New operators can apply for both existing locations for shared use &/or new locations, both of which will be subject for review to determine if the location is feasible.


To apply for a New York City Bus Stop visit the NYCStreets Permit Management System<https://www.nycstreets.net/Public/SignIn/Index> website. Note, registration is required before you can submit an application. You will need to following documents on had to register each bus operator:


  *   Employer Identification Number Registration (EIN as provided by the IRS)
  *   Legal Name Certificate Proof of registration provided by Department of State (i.e. Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Organization & Certificate of Publication, or Certificate of Limited Partnership & Certificate of Publication, Proof of registration provided by Clerk of County (i.e. Business Certificate for Partners or Business Certificate)
  *   Doing Business As Certificate (if applicable)
  *   Sightseeing License (as issued by the NYC DCWP)
  *   Entity Address
  *   US DOT Number https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration
  *   NYSDOT
  *   MC Number
  *   Automobile Insurance Certificate (1-page COI)
  *   Vehicle Vin List with matching Registration & Insurance ID Cards for all vehicles
  *   Primary Contact Phone Number
  *   Emergency Phone Number (must differ from primary)


If you have any additional questions, please contact NYC DOT Bus Stop Permit Office at: ICBPSAdmin@dot.nyc.gov<mailto:ICBPSAdmin@dot.nyc.gov> or (646) 892-1289.
