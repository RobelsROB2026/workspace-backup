# Official Contacts for NYC Sightseeing Bus Business

## 1. NYC Department of Transportation (DOT)
*Key for: Bus Stop Permits, Route Approval*
- **General Permits Office:** (646) 892-1242
- **Website:** https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/motorist/bus-permits.shtml

## 2. NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP)
*Key for: Bus License, Guide License, Inspections*
- **Business Compliance Counsel:** BCC@dcwp.nyc.gov
- **Licensing Center (Manhattan):** 42 Broadway, Lobby
- **Website:** https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/description/sightseeing-bus-license

## 3. NYS Department of Transportation (State Level)
*Key for: Safety Inspections, Operating Authority*
- **Inspection Unit:** (718) 482-4754
- **Website:** https://www.dot.ny.gov/index

## 4. NYS Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)
