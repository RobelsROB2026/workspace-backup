# Requirements for NYC Sightseeing Bus Business

## 1. DCWP Sightseeing Bus License
Required for any vehicle carrying 8+ paying passengers.
- **Cost:** ~$100/bus for 2 years (varies by filing date).
- **Expiration:** March 31 of even years.
- **Documents:**
    - Basic License Application.
    - Roster of Buses.
    - Proof of Insurance.
    - Rate Schedule (must be filed with DCWP).
    - **Headphone System:** Open-air buses *must* have headphone-limited sound systems. Loudspeakers are banned.

## 2. Bus Stop Permits (The Hard Part)
You cannot stop just anywhere. You need a "NYC DOT Authorized Sightseeing Bus Stop Permit" for *each* on-street stop.
- **Issuing Agency:** NYC DOT.
- **Cost:** ~$520 application fee per stop.
- **Constraint:** Stops are scarce and highly contested. This is the main barrier to entry.

## 3. Vehicle Requirements
- **Emissions:** Must meet USEPA Engine Conformity (Local Law 41).
- **Inspection:** DCWP inspection every 4 months.
- **NYS DOT/DMV:** Valid inspection certificate (DOT every 6 months, or DMV every year).

## 4. Personnel
- **Drivers:** Valid NYS Commercial Driver's License (CDL).
- **Guides:** Must hold a "Sightseeing Guide License" from DCWP.
    - Requires passing a 150-question exam (Score 97+).
    - In-person exam in Manhattan.
