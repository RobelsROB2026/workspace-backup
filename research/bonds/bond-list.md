# Texas Surety Bonds List

## County-Specific Bonds (High Value / Low Competition)
These are rare and specific. Great for long-tail SEO.

- **Fort Bend County:** Performance Bond (Cable/Pole lines in right-of-way)
- **Galveston County:** Right-of-Way Bond ($100,000 for pipelines/utilities)
- **Harris County:** Construction of Facilities in Rights-of-Way Bond
- **Travis County:** Right-of-Way Permit Bond (Driveway access)

## City-Specific Bonds (Municipal Requirements)
Over 100 cities have specific contractor bond requirements.

**Key Examples:**
- **Houston:** Towing Service Private Storage Lot Bond, Permit Bonds
- **El Paso:** Foreign Trade Zone User Bond, Contractor Bond
- **San Antonio:** Contractor Bond
- **Dallas:** Surety Bonds (various)
- **Wichita Falls:** Precious Metals Dealer Bond, Contractor Compliance Bond
- **Sherman:** Junk / Secondhand Dealer Bond
- **Abilene, Amarillo, Beaumont, Lubbock, Midland, Waco:** Contractor Bonds
- **Coastal Cities (Galveston, Port Aransas, South Padre Island):** Construction Debris & Building Contractor Bonds
