## Key Insights

1. **Opus 4.5 is 3x cheaper than Opus 4** - $5/$25 vs $15/$75. We're already on the best deal.

2. **Gemini has generous free tiers** - Most Gemini models work free of charge for development.

3. **Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite is insanely cheap** - $0.10/$0.40 per MTok. Good for bulk processing.

4. **For research synthesis:**
   - Gemini 2.5 Flash: $0.30 input / $2.50 output (free tier available)
   - vs Opus 4.5: $5 input / $25 output
   - **Gemini is ~10-17x cheaper** for summarization work

5. **Google Search grounding** costs extra ($14-35 per 1K searches) but has free monthly allowance.
