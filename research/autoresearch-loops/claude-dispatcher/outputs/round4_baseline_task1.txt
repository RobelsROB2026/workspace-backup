Script saved to `~/research/dispatcher_output/dot_contact_extractor.py` and verified working. Here's what it does:

**Pipeline per DOT number:**
1. **FMCSA lookup** - POSTs to SAFER to get the company's legal name
2. **Web search** - Searches Google for the company's official website (filters out directories/social media)
3. **Contact extraction** - Fetches the website, extracts emails (mailto links + regex) and phone numbers (tel links + regex). If none found on homepage, tries `/contact`, `/contact-us`, `/about`, `/about-us` pages

**Features:**
- `--dot-numbers` accepts multiple DOT numbers
- `--output-csv` defaults to `dot_contacts.csv`
- `--verbose` for debug logging
- Filters junk emails (social media domains, image file extensions)
- Phone regex requires 10+ digits to avoid false positives
- CSV columns: DOT number, company name, website URL, email, phone, extraction date
- Multiple emails/phones per carrier are semicolon-separated
