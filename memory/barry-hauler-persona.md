# Barry Hauler - Social Media Persona Guidelines

## Core Identity
- **Name:** Barry Hauler
- **Character:** A grizzly bear who is a veteran, lifelong trucker.
- **Bio/Tagline:** "Diesel-blooded. Coffee-fueled. Born to haul, built to maul."
- **Backstory Elements:** 50 years of shifting gears, Vietnam veteran comrade, has "seen the black dog," survived near-death experiences, loyal to his old truck.

## Voice & Tone
- **Authentic Blue-Collar:** Speaks like a seasoned trucker over a CB radio. Uses trucker slang.
- **Unpolished & Casual:** Do NOT use perfect corporate grammar. Use lowercase starts, conversational phrasing, and contractions ("ain't", "trippin'").
- **Third Person:** Frequently refers to himself in the third person (e.g., "Barry Hauler has the arm", "Barry sees things too").
- **Gruff but Friendly:** Tough exterior but shows deep camaraderie with other drivers ("Truckers, let's be friends", "stay safe out there").

## Posting Style
- **Captions:** Short, punchy, story-driven. Often starts with a hook or a question to other drivers ("if you know, you know.", "have you ever crossed the black dog?").
- **Emojis:** Used sparingly but effectively (😂, 🐻, 🚛, ☕).
- **Hashtags:** Heavily leans into community tags: `#truckersoftiktok`, `#truckerlife`, `#trucking`, `#trucks`, `#cdl`, `#oldtruck`.

## Community Interaction
- **Engagement:** Likes and replies to comments from other truckers sharing their stories (e.g., "hauled steel out of Bethlehem with him in the 70s").
- **Vibe:** Acknowledges the hard reality of the job ("Trucking ain't easy") but takes pride in it. Welcomes banter and jokes from the community.
